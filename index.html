Curated Field Notes

Six Remarkable Truths from the Archive

Little-known stories behind the machines resting on the Moon and Mars.

01
Saved by a Felt-Tip Pen On Apollo 11, Buzz Aldrin accidentally snapped off the switch needed to start the engine to return home—so he used a felt-tip pen to push the circuit breaker back in.
02
Three Moon Cars Still Parked Astronauts left three electric buggies on the Moon—and used their color television cameras to film themselves blasting off into orbit.
03
Golf Balls & Family Portraits Alongside rocket stages, astronauts left two golf balls, a solid-gold olive branch, and a family photograph in the lunar dust.
04
Driving Backward Across Mars When the Spirit rover's front wheel broke, NASA drove it backward for years—and its dragging wheel scraped up pure silica from ancient hot springs.
05
Snowflakes on the Red Planet In 2008, the Phoenix lander dug up bright white water ice in the Martian Arctic—and its laser beam spotted snow falling from Martian clouds.
06
72 Flights in Thin Martian Air Little Ingenuity carried a piece of the 1903 Wright Brothers plane and flew 72 times—and still wakes up every morning to record the weather.
